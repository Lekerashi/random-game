How to Play

  1. Pick your starting station — GPS, search, or random.
  2. Roll the dice to pick a train line and direction.
  3. Head to the platform and board the next train.
  4. Roll again right away — ride that many stops.
  5. If you pass a transfer station, roll to decide: switch lines, stay on, or get off and drink!
  6. Arrive at your stop, get off, and grab a drink!
  7. Head back to the station and go again!

Explore Tokyo and discover new places to drink!

  • When you arrive at a station, follow a crowd of people until you find a place that you want to try drinking at.
  • Wandered a bit and ended up closer to another station? Roll from that one instead after you drink.
  • The game is optimized to be played within the 23 wards of Tokyo, but is still fun throughout the greater Tokyo area.
  • To optimize for more drinking or to not end up too far when last train is approaching, you can cap the max stops you can roll. Between 5 and 10 is usually the sweet spot.
